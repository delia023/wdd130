Nigeria

Nigeria is a country located in West Africa. Its capital city is Abuja, and it is one of the most populated countries in Africa. Nigeria is known for its rich cultural diversity, different languages, traditional festivals, music, and delicious food. The country has beautiful landscapes, natural resources, and friendly people.
